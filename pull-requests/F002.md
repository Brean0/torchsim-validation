# Split weighted pair energies equally between atoms

## Problem and result

`PairPotentialModel` adds each weighted pair energy to the system once and to each endpoint in full. As a result, the sum of `energies` is twice the returned system `energy`, for both full and half neighbor lists.

For two isolated soft-sphere atoms at distance 0.5, with epsilon=sigma=1 and alpha=2, the system energy is 0.125 but the atom energies are `[0.125, 0.125]`. Allocate half of the weighted pair energy to each endpoint, giving `[0.0625, 0.0625]` and preserving the system total of 0.125.

## Reproduction

```sh
python -m pytest -q tests/models/test_pair_potential.py -k 'isolated_pair_atom_energies or batched_atom_energies_unequal_system_sizes'
```

The four isolated-pair cases and four unequal-batch cases fail at the expected per-atom energy assertion with baseline accumulation and pass with the fix. Both fixtures cover full and half lists with `torch_nl_n2` and the default `torchsim_nl` backend on CPU float64. The unequal batch has three atoms in one system and two in the other; its expected atom energies are `[0.0625, 0.125, 0.0625, 0.0625, 0.0625]` and its system totals are `[0.25, 0.125]`. Each system's atom energies must sum to its own returned total.

## Scope and evidence

This change is limited to per-atom energy allocation and regressions. It leaves the system energy, force accumulation, stress accumulation, and neighbor-list construction unchanged. GPU and long-running simulation validation were not performed.

The [F002 evidence and native ASE control](https://github.com/Brean0/torchsim-validation/blob/main/docs/findings/F002.md) documents the original invariant failure. The evidence repository is private while this draft is under review; make that link accessible before publication.
