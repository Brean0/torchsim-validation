## Proposed pull request body

Soft-sphere energy can be zero while its forces are NaN when a fractional exponent is used outside a pair's interaction support. For example, with sigma `[[1,2],[2,2]]`, alpha 2.5, species `[18,18,36]`, and positions at x=`[0,1.5,5]`, the global neighbor cutoff retains an Ar–Ar pair beyond its own sigma. The current implementation raises a negative base to a fractional power before masking the energy. Differentiation then propagates NaNs through the masked branch.

This change substitutes a positive dummy base outside `dr < sigma` before exponentiation and preserves the final zero-energy mask. It updates both `soft_sphere_pair` and `MultiSoftSpherePairFn.forward`. The mixed-species example now returns zero energy and finite zero forces. Regression tests check scalar pair gradients inside and strictly outside support for alpha 0.5 and 2.5. A four-atom mixed-species control keeps one active pair beside retained neighbors outside their species support and checks its known nonzero energy and forces.
