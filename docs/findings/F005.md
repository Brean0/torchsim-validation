# F005 — Unequal-mass initialization uses a different zero-momentum projection

**Evidence level: qualified convention/distribution finding.** TorchSim and native JAX MD share one rule; native ASE `Stationary(preserve_temperature=False)` uses another. Both rules remove total momentum and center-of-mass velocity. The difference concerns the projection and resulting distribution. This evidence does **not** establish a failure to remove COM motion or unanimous cross-library disagreement.

For raw Gaussian momenta p, total P, particle masses m_i and total mass M:

- TorchSim subtracts **P/N** from each particle's momentum.
- ASE subtracts **m_i P/M**, applying one common velocity shift.

Use masses **[1,4]**, kT=1, Torch generator seed=4, and reconstruct the identical raw Gaussian momenta with a separate generator at the same seed. Pass that raw sample to native ASE with temperature preservation disabled, keeping extra rescaling out of the comparison. The maximum momentum difference is **0.7901322663896455**. Both returned total momenta are [0,2.22e-16,0]. The native ASE result matches the common-velocity formula with **zero maximum error**.

If the intended distribution is canonical Gaussian momenta conditioned on total momentum zero, the conditional covariance in one coordinate is `diag(m_i*kT) - kT*m*m.T/M`. The common-velocity projection produces that covariance. For this two-particle case, each particle's conditional momentum variance is **0.8**. The arithmetic-mean-momentum projection instead gives variance **(1+4)/4=1.25** for both particles. A live TorchSim calculation over **32,768 independent systems**, three coordinates each, seed=71, measures **1.2510351317695345** and **1.2510351317695347**, consistent with its implemented projection. Whether this distribution is a concern depends on the intended constrained thermal distribution. Total-momentum removal alone leaves the choice of rule open.

## Recommended clarification and conditional change (proposed)

First state the intended constrained ensemble and drift-removal convention in `initialize_momenta` at `torch_sim/integrators/md.py`, audited lines 113–165. The current rule removes total momentum and is shared by the measured native JAX MD control. The ASE difference alone does not justify an immediate universal bug fix.

If the intended result is Gaussian canonical momenta conditioned on zero total momentum, replace the arithmetic-mean-momentum subtraction with a common center-of-mass velocity subtraction. For each system s, compute total momentum `P_s` and total mass `M_s`, then apply `p_i -= m_i * P_s / M_s` to its particles. Retain or explicitly revise the existing single-particle exception according to that stated convention.

Under that assumption, regressions should verify zero total momentum and the unequal-mass conditional covariance, including the expected variance 0.8 for masses [1,4] at kT=1. If a different ensemble is intended, document it and test its distribution instead. No projection change has been applied to the audited source or the saved measurements.
